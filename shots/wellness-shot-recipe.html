Method

  1. 01
    Prep all ingredients
    Wash all produce thoroughly. Peel and roughly chop the ginger (140g) and fresh turmeric (70g). Peel and quarter the 4 oranges. Juice all 6 lemons and set aside.
    ⏱ 5 min
  2. 02
    Blend on high
    Add the water, olive oil, and lemon juice to a high-powered blender first. Then add the orange quarters, ginger, and turmeric. Blend on high for 45–60 seconds until completely smooth and vibrant gold.
    ⏱ 1 min
  3. 03
    Strain
    Pour through a fine mesh strainer or nut milk bag into a large jug. Press or squeeze firmly to extract every drop. Discard or freeze the pulp for smoothies. Note: turmeric stains — use an old cloth.
    ⏱ 3 min
  4. 04
    Season & bottle
    Whisk in the freshly cracked black pepper. Pour into 14 × 45ml sealed bottles, or divide between two airtight jars (one per person). Label with today's date.
    ⏱ 2 min
  5. 05
    Store & serve
    Refrigerate for up to 7 days. Shake well before each use — ingredients will settle. Take one 45ml shot per person daily, ideally on an empty stomach first thing in the morning.
    ✓ Best on empty stomach

Tips & upgrades

Black pepper — essential A pinch per shot boosts turmeric's curcumin absorption by up to 2,000%. Never skip it.
Olive oil — bioavailability Curcumin is fat-soluble. The oil ensures your body can actually absorb and use it.
Too spicy? Add a small drizzle of raw honey to individual servings to balance the heat from ginger.
Freeze for longer Pour into silicone ice cube trays and freeze for up to 3 months. Drop a cube in hot water for a warming tonic.
Best timing Take on an empty stomach in the morning for maximum absorption and a natural energy boost.
Staining warning Turmeric stains everything — hands, clothes, cutting boards. Use gloves and old utensils when blending.
